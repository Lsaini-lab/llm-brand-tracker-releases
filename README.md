## Why LLM Brand Monitoring Matters

Generative AI is replacing traditional search for product recommendations. ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini now answer questions like *"best CRM for startups"* or *"top project management tools"* — and they cite specific brands. If your brand isn't in those answers, you're invisible to a fast-growing share of your market.

**LLM Brand Tracker gives you the data to fix that.** It shows you:
- Whether your brand is mentioned at all across each AI platform
- Your rank position among all brands cited
- Your Share of Voice % (how often you appear vs. competitors)
- Which competitors AI recommends instead of you
- How your visibility changes week over week

This is the foundation of **Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)** — optimizing your brand's presence in AI-generated answers, not just traditional search results.

## What Is Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)?

**GEO** (also called AEO — Answer Engine Optimization) is the practice of optimizing your brand's presence in AI-generated answers. As ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini become primary research tools, appearing in their responses is as valuable as ranking on Google page 1.

GEO involves:
- Publishing authoritative content that LLMs cite
- Ensuring your brand is mentioned alongside category keywords
- Tracking brand share of voice across AI platforms over time
- Measuring competitive position in AI-generated recommendations

LLM Brand Tracker is the measurement layer for your GEO strategy — it tells you where you stand today and whether your efforts are working.

## How It Works

1. **Setup wizard** — enter your brand name, keywords, and connect Google Sheets (optional)
2. **Browser login** — the app opens Chrome and navigates to each AI platform; you log in once; sessions last 30–60 days
3. **Run** — click Run Now (or schedule); the app queries each platform with your keyword and extracts every brand mentioned
4. **Results** — Dashboard shows brand rank, share of voice, and platform breakdown; Google Sheets gets a new row per run

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Does this work with the free tier of ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini?**
Yes. Free accounts on all platforms work fine.

**How accurate is brand detection?**
~80% accuracy with no API key (known-brand scan). Add an Anthropic API key in Settings to boost to ~90% (~$0.001/run via Claude Haiku).

**How often should I run it?**
Weekly is typical for trend tracking. Daily if you're running active GEO campaigns.

**Does AI mention my brand? How do I find out?**
Run LLM Brand Tracker with your category keyword (e.g., "best CRM for startups"). The dashboard shows whether each AI platform mentioned you and at what rank position.

**Why do some platforms show "Login expired"?**
Browser sessions expire every 30–60 days. Go to Settings → Browser Sessions → Re-run Login Setup to refresh. Grok expires fastest (~2–4 weeks).

**Is my data private?**
All data stays on your machine and in your own Google Sheet. No data is sent to any server we control.

**Is it free?**
Yes — the app is free. The optional Anthropic API key costs ~$0.001 per run for improved accuracy.

**Can I track multiple keywords?**
Yes — add them on the Keywords page and click "Run All Keywords" to process each in sequence.

**What is LLM SEO?**
LLM SEO (also called GEO or AEO) refers to strategies that improve how often and how prominently an LLM like ChatGPT or Claude recommends your brand when users ask about your product category. LLM Brand Tracker measures your current LLM SEO performance.
