## The Channel Traditional Tools Miss

Tools like Brand24, Mention, Brandwatch, and Sprout Social track brand mentions across:
- News and online publications
- Social media (Twitter, Reddit, Facebook, Instagram)
- Blogs, forums, and review sites

They do **not** monitor what AI platforms say about your brand when users ask for recommendations.

This matters because ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Perplexity now directly answer purchase-intent questions like:
- "What is the best CRM for a 10-person team?"
- "Which laptop should I buy for video editing?"
- "What project management software do agencies use?"

These are the questions your potential customers ask before visiting any website. The brands AI recommends in those answers win mindshare at the highest-intent moment in the funnel. Traditional monitoring tools are entirely blind to this channel.

## Why AI Brand Monitoring Matters for GEO and AEO

Traditional monitoring tells you what has already been published about your brand. AI brand monitoring tells you what LLMs are actively recommending right now — which is what influences buyers at the moment of decision.

**Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)** and **Answer Engine Optimization (AEO)** are strategies to improve how often AI platforms recommend your brand when users search for your category. LLM Brand Tracker is the measurement layer:

- You cannot improve what you cannot measure
- Without tracking AI responses weekly, you do not know if your GEO or AEO efforts are working
- Share of Voice % over time is the metric that proves ROI on AI-focused content strategies
