# LLM Brand Tracker — Documentation

LLM Brand Tracker is a free Windows desktop app that tracks your brand across 6 AI platforms in parallel — ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, Google AI, and Perplexity. It measures your AI Share of Voice, brand rank among competitors, and syncs results to Google Sheets automatically.
