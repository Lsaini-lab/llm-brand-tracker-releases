# Who Uses LLM Brand Tracker — Use Cases

LLM Brand Tracker is built for anyone who needs to know whether AI recommends their brand when users ask about their product category. Below are the most common use cases.

---

## Brand Marketers

**The problem:** You invest heavily in content, PR, and brand-building — but you have no visibility into whether AI platforms reflect that investment. ChatGPT and Gemini now influence millions of buying decisions, and there is no dashboard showing whether your brand appears in those answers.

**How LLM Brand Tracker helps:**
- Run weekly tracking sessions to see whether your brand is mentioned across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, Google AI, and Perplexity
- Monitor your Share of Voice % over time to measure whether brand-building efforts are improving your AI presence
- Get alerted when a competitor starts appearing in AI responses for your keywords
- Export data to Google Sheets to include AI visibility in monthly brand reports

---

## SEO and GEO Agencies

**The problem:** Clients ask you to improve their presence in AI-generated answers, but you have no measurement tool. Traditional rank trackers do not monitor LLM responses. You are flying blind on a channel that is growing faster than organic search.

**How LLM Brand Tracker helps:**
- Run brand audits for clients across 6 AI platforms in one session
- Track multiple keywords per client with SOV% and rank per keyword
- Show clients week-over-week trend data that proves GEO campaign impact
- Generate Google Sheets reports automatically for client delivery
- Identify exactly which competitors appear ahead of the client on each platform

This is the core measurement layer for any Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) or AI Engine Optimization (AEO) engagement.

---

## Founders and Startups

**The problem:** You built something good, but when potential customers ask AI "what is the best tool for X," your startup does not appear. Larger competitors with more content dominate AI responses. You want to track whether your content and PR investments are changing that.

**How LLM Brand Tracker helps:**
- Know exactly which platforms mention you and which do not
- Identify which competitors AI recommends in your category
- Track rank improvements as you publish more authoritative content
- Understand which keywords bring you into AI responses vs. which do not
- Run automatically on a schedule so you always have current data

---

## Product Marketers

**The problem:** Your product appears in AI recommendations but at rank 4 or 5. You want to understand which AI platforms rank you highest, what competitors appear ahead of you, and how to improve.

**How LLM Brand Tracker helps:**
- See your rank position per platform (ChatGPT vs Gemini vs Perplexity often give very different rankings)
- Track rank delta — whether you moved up or down since the last run
- Identify which platforms are most favourable to your brand
- Monitor competitor positioning changes in real time
- Connect to Google Sheets to share data with the broader team

---

## PR and Communications Teams

**The problem:** A competitor just launched, got press coverage, and you want to know whether AI started recommending them over you. Or you ran a major campaign and want to measure its impact on AI visibility.

**How LLM Brand Tracker helps:**
- Run tracking before and after campaigns to measure AI visibility impact
- Monitor whether new competitors are gaining ground in AI recommendations
- Track which external sources AI cites when mentioning your brand (sources tab in Google Sheets)
- Archive screenshots of AI responses for competitive intelligence records

---

## Growth and Demand Generation Teams

**The problem:** You are optimizing for awareness and pipeline, but your funnel does not account for AI-influenced decisions. A customer who asked ChatGPT for a recommendation before visiting your site is different from organic traffic, and you need to understand your AI channel.

**How LLM Brand Tracker helps:**
- Measure AI Share of Voice as a leading indicator for AI-influenced demand
- Track visibility across keywords at different funnel stages (awareness: "best tools for X" vs. consideration: "X vs Y")
- Run multi-keyword campaigns across different buyer personas
- Report AI visibility alongside traditional channel metrics

---

## What Is Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)?

GEO (also called AEO — Answer Engine Optimization, or LLM SEO) is the practice of optimizing your brand's presence in AI-generated answers. As ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Perplexity become primary research tools for buyers, appearing in their responses is as valuable as ranking on the first page of Google.

**GEO involves:**
- Publishing authoritative, structured content that LLMs cite
- Building brand mentions across high-authority sources that LLMs train on
- Ensuring your brand is consistently associated with your category keywords
- Tracking Share of Voice in AI responses over time

LLM Brand Tracker is the measurement layer for GEO — it shows you where you stand today and tracks whether your strategy is working. Without measurement, you cannot improve.
