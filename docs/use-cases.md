# Who Uses LLM Brand Tracker

Anyone who needs to know whether AI recommends their brand when users search for their category. Here are the most common situations.

---

## Brand Marketers

You invest in content, PR, and brand-building — but you have no visibility into whether any of that shows up in AI-generated answers. ChatGPT and Gemini now influence buying decisions before a user visits any website.

LLM Brand Tracker gives you a weekly number to track: your AI Share of Voice %. Run it every Monday, watch the trend in Google Sheets, and you'll know whether your strategy is moving the needle — or whether a competitor is quietly gaining ground in AI responses while you're looking elsewhere.

---

## SEO and GEO Agencies

Clients are asking about GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) and you need measurement tooling. Traditional rank trackers don't touch LLM responses. This does.

Run a brand audit for a new client across all 6 platforms in one session. Show them their current AI Share of Voice, which competitors appear ahead of them, and on which platforms. Then track weekly and show them the delta over time — that's your proof of work.

The app exports everything to Google Sheets automatically, so client reporting is just sharing a link.

---

## Founders and Startups

You built something, published content, did some PR — but when a potential customer asks ChatGPT what the best tool in your category is, you don't appear. Larger competitors with more content dominate AI responses.

The only way to change that is to first understand the baseline: which platforms mention you, at what rank, for which keywords. Then you track weekly to see if your efforts are shifting the numbers. LLM Brand Tracker gives you that baseline and the ongoing trend.

---

## Product Marketers

You're in the responses — but at rank 4. You want to know which platforms rank you highest, which rank competitors ahead of you, and whether that's changing over time.

The rank delta column in the trends sheet is useful here. If you're moving from rank 4 to rank 2 on Perplexity over 6 weeks after publishing a series of comparison articles, you can attribute that. If nothing is moving, you know to change tactics.

---

## PR and Communications Teams

You launched a campaign, a product, or got major press. Did it change anything in AI responses? 

Run a tracking session the week before and the week after. Screenshot the AI responses (the app captures them automatically per run). If your AI visibility jumped, you have data to attach to the campaign report. If it didn't, you know AI responses lag press mentions by a few weeks and can set expectations accordingly.

---

## Growth Teams

AI-influenced traffic is a real channel now. Buyers ask ChatGPT for recommendations before ever visiting your site — and those visitors convert differently than organic search traffic.

Tracking your AI Share of Voice by keyword gives you a leading indicator of AI-influenced demand. Run it across keywords at different funnel stages: awareness keywords ("best tools for X") and consideration keywords ("X vs Y"), and you'll see which keywords your brand actually wins in AI versus where you lose.

---

## What is GEO?

GEO (Generative Engine Optimization), also called AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) or LLM SEO, is the practice of improving your brand's presence in AI-generated answers. As ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity become primary research tools, appearing in their responses carries real value — not unlike ranking on page 1 of Google.

The playbook is still maturing, but the core levers are: publishing authoritative content that LLMs cite, building brand mentions across high-authority sources, and consistently associating your brand with the right category keywords.

LLM Brand Tracker is the measurement layer. You can't improve what you don't measure, and without tracking AI responses over time, you're guessing.
